### Reading the result

- The vendor reports 37% single-prompt accuracy on CIM data points. On Kestrel the single prompt scored 0.638 on truth and 0.707 as stated (above 37% on truth, by its interval). But the Kestrel CIM is short: 762 words of text on 11 pages (`data/dataroom/kestrel/export/records.jsonl`), so the whole document fits easily in one prompt. The vendor's figure is plausibly from long CIMs; this test cannot confirm or refute it at that length.
- Staged vs single: +0.121 [-0.034, +0.259] on truth after locate. The interval includes zero, so on this one short CIM staging is not shown to be better; the direction favours staging.
- Staged + reconciliation vs staged: +0.069 [+0.017, +0.138] on truth; the interval excludes zero. Reconciliation replaced CIM figures that Part 1 flagged (discrepancy or definition_mismatch) with the tier-3 values. As stated, it scores lower by construction (-0.052 [-0.121, +0.000]): it no longer reports what the CIM says for those fields.
- Cost of (c) = (b) + the Part 1 claims extraction (`facts:claims` ledger rows, which also cover the deck and transcript, so an upper bound); its latency adds the reconcile step only (the Part 1 extraction ran in the facts stage). Token counts include instructor's validation retries, which re-send the prompt (the single call used 20122 input tokens for one CIM).
- Locate step: quotes are matched verbatim (`Source.match: exact`), else by their tokens in order within one block (`in_order`, decision 2026-11-07 Q14). `in_order` can match across table rows: it guarantees the cited block contains the value, not that the value sits on the quoted label. Label errors show up as wrong values in the gold scoring.
- Raw vs after locate for (b): 0.707 [0.586, 0.828] raw vs 0.759 [0.638, 0.862] after locate. Raw is lower because CS1 split the page-3 highlights table into one block per cell; the staged call quoted those cells as "FY2023 Revenue 61.8" (gross revenue labelled revenue), which cannot be located and are dropped after locate, so the merge keeps the page-9 table's correctly labelled values.

## 3. Notes and known issues

- **Deal-only retrieval for the RAG memo** (decision 2026-11-08 Q2 b): the memo's retrieval keeps only `deal_id == kestrel` chunks, an extra filter beyond the brief's scope rule (a deal scope returns the deal's records plus public ones). It is used because comps are pending; revisit in Part 6. Before the filter, public 10-K text crowded into the target's sections: drafts stated another company's headcount (Payoneer's) and another company's CEO as if they were Kestrel's. Those claims were dropped by the reference check, not by design.
- **Non-number entries** (decision Q1 c): entries in a claim's numbers that hold no number the linter reads (names, titles, dates) are removed and a block-id reference moves to citations; every number in the text is still linted.
- **Disclosure Gaps rendering**: a claim "X is/are not disclosed." with no numbers is rendered as "- X: Not disclosed." (the brief's literal), by `render_gap` in `src/extract/memo.py`, with no extra LLM call.
- **Comps**: no comparable-company data in either payload (Part 6 pending, decision Q6 a); both memos say valuation context is pending.
- **Superseded memo calls**: 41 paid LLM calls (0.144612 USD) from the two earlier memo record runs (the first truncated at memo.max_tokens 1500, now 4096; the second before the decisions above) remain in `data/extract_cache/llm` unused, and their ledger rows stay as spent.
- **Ledger understates spend slightly**: one failed call (the max_tokens truncation) is not in the ledger, because `src/llm/client.py` logs only successful calls.
- Ledger total after Part 5: 0.560151 USD (`logs/cost_ledger.jsonl`).
- **Deal A**: memos for deal_a in both modes (brief §8) wait until the generator builds Deal A.
