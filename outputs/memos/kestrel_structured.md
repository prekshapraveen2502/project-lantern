# Screening memo: Project Kestrel (structured payload)

## Company Overview

Kestrel Pay, Inc. provides integrated payments and practice-management software to independent healthcare clinics across the United States.
The company is headquartered in Columbus, Ohio.
Kestrel was founded in 2016.
Dana Whitfield is the Chief Executive Officer and co-founded Kestrel in 2016.
Omar Feld is the Chief Financial Officer and joined the company in 2022.
The sale process is a majority recapitalization with a bid deadline of October 30, 2026.

## Business Model and Go-to-Market

Kestrel's revenue streams include subscription revenue and transaction revenue.
Kestrel sells through direct sales and a network of referral partners.
Management claims that 92% of the company's revenue is recurring (per CIM, unverified). [C-8933bd199abc; DR-kestrel-001:p0005_b002]
Seller financials show recurring revenue share of 29.3% (per seller financials). [CALC-88b7ee003052; DR-kestrel-005:s001]
Management claims the top partner accounts for 10% of net revenue (per CIM, unverified). [C-a5f8a57dadc2; DR-kestrel-001:p0006_b002]
Seller financials indicate the top partner's share is 16.8% (per seller financials). [F-2c3ae5e6293e; DR-kestrel-005:s004]
The top ten customers represent less than 30.2% of net revenue according to seller financials (per seller financials). [F-c0faadc11b94; DR-kestrel-005:s004]
Kestrel's average contract length is 36 months according to management (per CIM, unverified). [C-f4d99810ab02; DR-kestrel-001:p0005_b006]
