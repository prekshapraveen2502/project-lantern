# Screening memo: Project Kestrel (RAG-only)

## Company Overview

Kestrel provides integrated payments and practice-management software to independent healthcare clinics across the United States (per CIM, unverified). [DR-kestrel-001:p0002_b001]
Kestrel was founded in 2016 and is headquartered in Columbus, Ohio (per CIM, unverified). [DR-kestrel-001:p0002_b001]
The company sells through direct sales and a network of referral partners (per CIM, unverified). [DR-kestrel-001:p0002_b001]
Dana Whitfield is the Chief Executive Officer and co-founded Kestrel in 2016 (per CIM, unverified). [DR-kestrel-001:p0010_b001]
Omar Feld is the Chief Financial Officer and joined Kestrel in 2022 from a regional payments processor (per CIM, unverified). [DR-kestrel-001:p0010_b001]
Ridgeline Partners is managing the sale process and is seeking a majority recapitalization of the company (per CIM, unverified). [DR-kestrel-001:p0011_b001]
